# Brandolini's Reference

Source: Mark Qvist, Brandolini's Reference chapter, Reticulum
Network Stack manual (reticulum.network/manual/brandolinis.html).
Verified against the live page Sept 2026.

## Brandolini's law

The amount of energy needed to refute bullshit is an order of
magnitude bigger than the effort needed to produce it (Alberto
Brandolini, 2013). Machine generation pushed the production cost
toward zero, so the asymmetry is now the whole game.

Qvist's term is "bullshit asymmetry": producing a fluent,
plausibly-sourced smear or clone is the work of minutes. Refuting
it to documentation standard is the work of days, and the refuted
claim is replaced by the next within the week. The asymmetry is
not a side effect, it is the campaign. The goal is exhaustion.

## The patterns documented

- Weaponized concern: the register is always solicitude ("we are
  worried about the ecosystem"), and the wanted change is always
  the same: replace the reference's authority with the speaker's.
  "Concern is the costume. Capture is the choreography."
- Event seizure: any event is stripped of context and re-narrated
  as evidence of a pre-written story.
- The legitimacy circuit: LLM-generated articles cite LLM-generated
  ports, the ports are praised in the articles, the conference
  talks cite the forums. A closed, self-referential loop that reads
  as a groundswell to a newcomer.
- The prior-work minefield: machine-generated implementations
  flood the space with fake prior work, bury real efforts, and
  dissuade honest developers who think it has already been done.
- On AI ports: "637,000 lines of unreviewed code. The human cannot
  answer for the code, because the choices were never made by a
  human."

## The grounded-generation model

The chapter itself was produced openly with machine assistance:
lc managing a Q8_K_XL quant of DeepSeek v4 Flash on llama.cpp,
grounded in ~100,000 tokens of text Qvist wrote by hand over
eight years. The human directed the inquiry, structure, and
editorial decisions and answers for the output.

That is the legitimate version of AI-assisted writing: the
machine amplifies an accountable human voice anchored in a real
corpus. The illegitimate version is unaccountable output with a
conformal surface.

## What this means for writing

- "Reads as human" means "reads as accountable". Specifics that
  can be checked, a voice that could answer for its claims.
- Fluent-polite-hollow is the slop register, whether the target
  is prose or code.
- If you know the reader is applying Brandolini math, do not make
  them do it: lead with the checkable specifics.
